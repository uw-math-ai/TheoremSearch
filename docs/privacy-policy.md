# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-17

Theorem Search is designed to minimize the data we collect.

## What we store

- We store only the text of your search query.

## What we do not store

- We do not store account information.
- We do not store payment information.
- We do not store the content of your files.

## Why we store queries

We use stored queries to operate, monitor, and improve search quality and reliability.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your query data. We may share limited data only when required by law.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please open an issue in this repository.
