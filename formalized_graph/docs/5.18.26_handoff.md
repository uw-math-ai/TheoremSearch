## add-combi failure — detailed

**Symptom**: project row exists in `corpus_v2.db` with **0 nodes**, despite a 38 MB / 31,632-line NDJSON on disk.

**Two complementary causes:**

1. **Extract was incomplete.** All 31,632 entries in `add-combi.ndjson` live in the `Init.*` namespace (Lean core). Not a single declaration is in `AddCombi.*` (the project's own namespace). At extract time on May 8, lean-graph could only resolve Lean-core oleans; the project's own `AddCombi.olean` was missing or stale, so the `--to AddCombi` traversal never reached the project's modules.

2. **Ingest dedupe.** Even those 31,632 `Init.*` names are already in Mathlib's transitive olean set, so `INSERT OR IGNORE ON full_name` attributed every row to Mathlib's `project_id` (first ingester wins). add-combi's `project_id` therefore has zero rows.

**Context — why the May 8 batch left add-combi looking superficially OK**: the May 8 extraction attempt (`run_extract_projects.sh`, job 35123856) ran lean-graph against pre-existing oleans for 11 projects. 10 produced empty NDJSONs with "incompatible header" errors (April 2026 oleans built with an older toolchain). add-combi was the one project whose pre-existing oleans happened to be readable — but only its `Init.*` exports came through; the project's own modules either weren't built or weren't on `LEAN_PATH`.

**Rebuild plan (in progress, not yet sbatched)**:

- Source: `https://github.com/YaelDillies/add-combi`
- Cloned to `data/formalization_projects/add-combi/`, currently checked out at commit `e91ebd8` ("Bump mathlib to v4.29.0")
- `lean-toolchain` = `leanprover/lean4:v4.29.0`; Mathlib pinned at `8a178386ffc0` (**exact match** to our `mathlib4` workspace)
- Next step: SLURM build job (`pipeline_a_v429_addcombi.sh`, untracked), then re-extract via `run_extract_projects.sh` array task 0, then delete the existing `add-combi` rows from corpus and re-ingest.
